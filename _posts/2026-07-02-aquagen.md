Molecules are not static. Atoms move, solvent matters — and ignoring this creates misleading confidence in drug discovery predictions. AquaGen is a generative AI framework for small-molecule motion at all-atom resolution with explicit water modeling, running significantly faster than traditional molecular dynamics. Physics-grounded, force-field-compatible outputs, with confidence scoring built in. This is the next generation of AI drug discovery infrastructure.
